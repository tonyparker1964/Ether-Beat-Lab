Arrangement

Clip

The song's patterns run along the top: click a block to change it, right-click to remove it, + Pattern to add one. Drag audio files onto the timeline, use Import audio or ● Record audio, and + Automation for a lane that moves the filter, reverb, echo or a volume over time.

Piano roll

Click to add a note (drag right as you click to make it longer). Drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to change its length, ⌥-drag to copy it, double-click or right-click to delete. Selected note: ↑ ↓ pitch (⇧ octave), ← → move, ⌥ ← → length, Delete removes. The ♪ button on the Melody, Bass and Chords rows of the Pattern grid opens them here.

Mixer

Drag a knob or fader up and down (hold Shift for fine moves), scroll on it, or click it and use the arrow keys. Double-click resets. M mutes, S solos.

PRESS PLAY

Saves a .zip with beat.wav (44.1 kHz stereo) and beat-markers.json for Ether Cut (tempo, chords, sections, and every beat, bar, kick and snare time). Stems are one WAV per track that line up from the start in any DAW; MIDI has the drums, bass, chords and melody as notes. It sounds just like the Mixer (EQ, compression, pans, sends and solos); muted tracks stay out.
